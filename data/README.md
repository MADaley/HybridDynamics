# Data

`swingLegKinematics_Blum2011.csv`: grand means of measured swing-leg kinematics at touchdown for running humans (7 subjects, treadmill) and pheasants (5 adult males, runway), from Tables 1 and 2 of Blum Y, Birn-Jeffery A, Daley MA, Seyfarth A (2011) Does a crouched leg posture enhance running stability and robustness? J Theor Biol 281:97-106, doi:10.1016/j.jtbi.2011.04.029 (values transcribed from the accepted author manuscript, HAL hal-00708520).

Columns: leg rotation rate at touchdown in degrees per gait cycle (deg/T, with T the gait cycle duration) and its standard deviation; leg length change in leg lengths per gait cycle (negative: shortening); ground speed matching GSM = 1 - v_foot / v_CoM at touchdown, in percent; normalized maximum drop (NMD) from the extended estimate of that paper, which includes the leg length change. Pheasant GSM and NMD were not transcribed. The touchdown angle is the measured angle of attack.
