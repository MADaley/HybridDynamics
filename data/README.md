# Data

`swingLegKinematics_Blum2011.csv`: grand means of measured swing-leg kinematics at touchdown for running humans (7 subjects, treadmill) and pheasants (5 adult males, runway), from Tables 1 and 2 of Blum Y, Birn-Jeffery A, Daley MA, Seyfarth A (2011) Does a crouched leg posture enhance running stability and robustness? J Theor Biol 281:97-106, doi:10.1016/j.jtbi.2011.04.029. Values checked against the published article.

Columns: leg length and Froude number of each speed group; leg rotation rate at touchdown in degrees per gait cycle (deg/T, with T the gait cycle duration) and its standard deviation; leg length change in leg lengths per gait cycle (negative: shortening); ground speed matching GSM = 1 - v_foot / v_CoM at touchdown, in percent, with standard deviation; normalized maximum drop (NMD) from the extended, model-based estimate of that paper, which includes the leg length change, with standard deviation. The touchdown angle is the measured angle of attack.
